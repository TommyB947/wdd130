Los Dientes del Diablo

Los Dientes del Diablo is our yealy special rafting location. It is a fun, high energy adventure only for the most comitted rafters. This two week trip will take you through tone of the highest intensity rafting experince that we have to offer.

Big Mallard

Big Mallard, is a great option for those who prefer a dense jungle rafting adventure. The trip is a specially designed three day trip. During this trip you will get to explore the dense expanse of the amazon rainforest.

Half Day Adventure

Our Half Day Adventure is perfect for those who want to go rafting but only for couple of hours. We will quickly get you geared up so you can get into the rapids.

Full Day Adventure

Our full day adventure is perfect for a adventerous weekend getaway. We provide a campsite with cabins in order for you day to be wonderful. You can customize your Full Day Adventure experince for one to three days. At Dry Ore Rafting, your needs are our greatest concern.

Week Long Adventure

Our Week Long Adventure here at Dry Ore is perfetc for any individual or family who is intrested in physical adventure. With options for both low intensity or high intensity rafting rivers, we have the place for everyone!
